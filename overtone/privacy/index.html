    Overtone privacy policy

    Overtone is an iPhone and Apple Watch app that plays a guide track over audio from other apps. It doesn’t collect, send or share any personal data: there are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

    • Your audio and library (playlists, episodes, play history and notes) stay on your iPhone, and on your Apple Watch if you use the watch app.
    • Health: with your permission, Overtone records workouts in Apple Health and reads your heart rate and active energy during them. This data stays in Health on your devices and is never sent anywhere else.
    • Downloads: if you choose Kokoro voices, the app downloads the voice model from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). As with any download, Hugging Face sees your IP address; nothing about you or your library is sent.
    • TestFlight: if you send feedback or the app crashes, Apple passes the feedback, the crash report, and your name and email from TestFlight to the developer. They’re used only to fix the app.

    Questions about this policy can be sent through TestFlight’s Send Beta Feedback, in the TestFlight app or by taking a screenshot in Overtone.
